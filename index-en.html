The Destination

Caraíva: Where time stands still and the soul rests.

Located in southern Bahia, the Village of Caraíva is one of Brazil's most preserved and authentic destinations. Often considered a pristine hidden gem Bahia has to offer, this car-free haven features sand streets and no public lighting, allowing only the starlight to guide you after a gentle canoe crossing over the river.

In Caraíva, luxury resides in peace and the feeling of sand beneath your feet. Between the river and the sea, the village preserves its rustic essence while gracefully embracing the concept of off-grid luxury Brazil is increasingly known for. It's the ideal destination for travelers seeking an authentic stay, where true sophistication is found in raw simplicity.

Whether you're watching the sunset by the river or diving into the warm Atlantic waters, our sanctuary elevates the experience of a secluded eco lodge Caraíva style, giving you the perfect setting to rediscover your own pace.

The Property

A 3,200m² sanctuary between the river and the sea.

Designed for travelers seeking a true escape, Quinta de Caraíva offers the quintessential boutique bungalow Bahia Brazil experience. Our four architectural bungalows are thoughtfully scattered across a vast, private tropical garden, blending seamlessly with nature. On one side, you are embraced by the calm serenity of the Caraíva River; on the other, the ocean breeze calls from just 120 meters away. Here, time passes slowly, and every detail invites you to rest.

The Journey

How to Get Here

Porto Seguro

The closest airport is Porto Seguro (BPS). From there, you can proceed by private transfer, taxi, or bus to the banks of the Caraíva River.

The Road

The trip takes about 2 to 3 hours. The dirt road requires extra attention and patience, especially on rainy days. If you choose to drive, check weather conditions before departing.

The Crossing

Upon reaching Nova Caraíva, you will cross the river in local canoes. For transfer arrivals, the drop-off is immediate at the crossing. For drivers, there are secure parking lots on-site.

Location

In the heart of Caraíva.

We are located in a privileged area of 3,200m², with views of the Caraíva River and just 120 meters from the beach. The perfect balance between village convenience and nature's peace.
